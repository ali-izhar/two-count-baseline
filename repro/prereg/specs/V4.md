# SPEC (v4). Section L: label corrections and label sensitivity (repro owner, part 1). Written 2026-10-27 before any rerun.

L1 MIT primary labels (our labels; corrected in the primary). Source: MIT Registrar, "Academic Calendar 2008-2009"
   (http://web.mit.edu/registrar/www/calendar.html, Wayback capture 20081018102435) and 5 U.S.C. 6103 (federal
   holidays). Rule: event date = the first day the calendar marks as holiday / vacation; for the end of the term, the
   first day after the LAST DAY OF CLASSES (Thu 14 May 2009) -> 15 May. Index = the first kept snapshot on or after the
   date. Columbus Day 2008-10-12 -> 2008-10-13 (index 19 -> 20); Thanksgiving 2008-11-26 -> 2008-11-27 (64 -> 65);
   Christmas 2008-12-25 (90, kept day 12-26; no kept snapshot 22-25 Dec); New Year 2009-01-01 (96); Spring Break
   2009-03-15 -> 2009-03-23 (168 -> 176); End of classes 2009-05-15 (229). Decided from the calendar alone, before any
   score with the new indices. Downstream: the F1 selection of steps 3, 8, 9 uses MIT; it is rerun with the new
   labels and its result is reported whichever way it goes (if a fixed setting changes, every dependent step reruns).
L2 Stablecoin (SCC) audit-corrected variant (sensitivity; the primary keeps SCPD Table 5). Rule (prereg DEVIATIONS 14,
   LABEL_AUDIT.md): each row's day = the earliest externally verified day on which it became public (governance vote:
   vote day; price move: day of the move); rows whose true day lies outside the series are removed; a row that
   repeats an earlier row's event (effective date of an announced action) is removed; rows the audit could not verify
   keep SCPD's date. Event = every distinct corrected day (as in the primary). Also a pool without SCC.
   Reported: pooled EP of every row on all ten and on >= 10 events with (a) SCPD labels, (b) audit labels, (c) without
   SCC; the counts minus every row (two-stage, Holm), and the turnover contrasts, for (b) and (c). Reading rule: a
   headline conclusion is "robust to the stablecoin labels" if its sign and its Holm significance status are the same
   in (a), (b) and (c); otherwise the paper states that it depends on SCC.
L3 UCI: primary keeps LAD's label. Report the pool without UCI (exists: pools.all_noUCI) beside the headline. Lag:
   the snapshot of the largest drop of active vertices and of edges in the 10 snapshots before the label, measured
   on the stored counts (descriptive only; no label change).

V3 Count variants as reference rows (series: step 22 section E, repro/data/settings/scores_<ID>.npz; EP: step 23):
   counts_w2_10 = the counts (two counts, MAD scale of the whole series, mode gam, weights 1 and 1/4) at w = 2,
   w_ref = 10 (the identity-aware rows' windows); counts_causal_edges = the edge count alone, scale = median |first
   difference| of the first 20 steps of the segment (as counts_causal), w = 2, w_ref = 5; counts_causal_expanding =
   the two counts, w = 2, w_ref = 5, gam (1, 1/4), each window difference divided at step t by the median |first
   difference| of the feature over the snapshots s0 .. t-1 of its segment (strictly past; floor 1e-12).
   Reported: EP all ten and >= 10 (two-stage CI); ASE, Hamming, NBS minus each causal variant on >= 10 (two-stage
   and t over datasets); the turnover contrasts unchanged. Reading: "best causal trivial baseline" = the causal
   variant (counts_causal, counts_causal_edges, counts_causal_expanding) with the highest pooled EP on ALL TEN
   (chosen on all ten, not on the pool where the gap is claimed). The paper states the >= 10 gap ASE - best causal
   and Hamming - best causal with their intervals; if an interval covers 0 the paper says the gap depends on the
   causal scale.
V4 Datasets (step 23 part N): per pooled dataset T, segments, tolerance, scored events, null windows on the common

V5 Small-cluster inference, >= 10-event pool (4 datasets): t_{3} interval over the 4 dataset means for every row's

V6 Plant effect sizes (step 14, plants.json "effect_table"): for every cell and row: paired AUC [CI], unpaired AUC
   (Mann-Whitney of planted vs original window maxima), mean increment in null-SD units, n_blocks; SCPD / ASE /
   SpotLight (stage X) get the unpaired AUC from their cached trials. Reading: a paired interval above 0.5 with
   unpaired AUC < 0.55 AND |increment| < 0.1 null SD is a "consistent but negligible" response (the paper may not
   call it detection without that qualifier); a background with < 10 blocks (PS 4, MIT 6, HT09 10?) is descriptive.
V7 Control cells (step 14 "controls"): per row, the number of relabelling cells (4 backgrounds) and of null cells
   (median moved share < 0.01: HT09 assort 0.1, triclose 0.1, 0.2, 0.5) whose paired 95% interval excludes 0.5,
   and the direction. Expected under calibrated intervals for an invariant row: 5% of cells.
V8 SCPD probe control (step 14 "scpd_probe"). SCPD's DOS uses Rademacher probes fixed per snapshot (RandomState(t))
   in vertex order; a relabelling applies a fresh draw of the same probe distribution. Control: realization k of
   the original background = probes RandomState(10^6 k + t), k = 0 the original. For negative realization a in
   0..9 and positive realizations b in 10..19 (10 per a, as the 10 plant seeds), trials as stage X (context
   [t0 - 12, t0) from a, window [t0, t0 + 5) from b, negative = realization a): paired AUC with the block CI per a,
   per background. Reading: if the PS relabelling cell (0.437) lies inside the range of the 10 re-probe paired
   AUCs on PS, and the share of re-probe intervals that exclude 0.5 exceeds 5%, the cell is explained by SCPD's
   probe noise with a fixed negative realization and few blocks (interval undercoverage), not by a response to the
   relabelling.
V9 LAD on its own generator (step 16 synth, identity_synth.json "lad_check"): LAD (methods/lad.py) on the 10 lad_sbm
