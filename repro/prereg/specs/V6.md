## C. Subject / case clustering in the within-dataset stage (DO-data 2; inference.pooled_draws level
## "three_stage"; step 23 part K)

Question: how much do the intervals widen when the events of one PAMAP2 subject (8 subjects) or one CHB-MIT case
(3 cases) are resampled together?
Method: three-stage paired bootstrap: datasets resampled with replacement; within each drawn dataset, its clusters
resampled with replacement; within each drawn cluster, its events resampled with replacement; the dataset mean is the
mean over all drawn events (equal weight per dataset as the headline). Datasets without clusters: every event its own
cluster (= the two-stage scheme). Variant K1: clusters = PM subjects (segment -> subject of PM_meta.json) and EEG
cases (eeg_case_of_step). Variant K2: K1 plus the day (segment) of PS (2) and HT09 (3). B = 10,000, seed 20261023.
Reported for the pools all ten and >= 10 events: EP interval of every row; counts - row and counts_causal - row with
the interval, bootstrap p and Holm F2; side by side with the two-stage numbers; the width ratio.
Reading (fixed now): reported as a SENSITIVITY, not the default: (i) EEG has 3 cases, so a case stage is itself a
3-cluster bootstrap with poor coverage; (ii) the default treats every dataset alike (events within datasets), and
K2 shows what consistent day-clustering of the multi-day datasets adds. The paper says which headline intervals and
p-values change (an interval that crosses 0 under K1 but not under two-stage is named).

## U. Simultaneous one-sided upper bounds for row - counts (DO-data 3; step 23 part U)

For each family F2 row r (16 rows) and reference c in {counts, counts_causal}, d_r = EP_r - EP_c; pools all ten
(primary) and >= 10 events (caveat: K = 4). Upper 95% bounds, simultaneous over the 16 rows:
  U1 max-t (two-stage draws): U_r = d_hat_r + c se_r, se_r the SD of the draws d*_r, c the 0.95 quantile over draws of
     max_r (d*_r - d_hat_r) / se_r;
  U2 Bonferroni (two-stage draws): U_r = the 1 - 0.05/16 quantile of d*_r (Holm gives no tighter bound);
  U3 dataset level, Bonferroni one-sided t_{K-1}: U_r = mean_r + t_{K-1, 1-0.05/16} s_r / sqrt(K);
  U4 dataset level, Bonferroni one-sided exact sign-flip: the largest shift delta not rejected by the one-sided
     sign-flip test of mean = delta against mean < delta at level 0.05/16 (all 2^K patterns; unbounded at K = 4).
Reported: every U_r and max_r U_r ("no row exceeds the counts by more than X").
