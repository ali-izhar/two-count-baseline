real data (UCI, Senate), k = 338 on the bills and k = 499 on its synthetic SBM. Two rows, both at LAD's fixed windows
(7, 10), threshold-free score (methods/lad.py unchanged):
  lad_k6    k = 6 (the code's and paper's real-data value);
  lad_k499  k = 499 (the code's synthetic value, under which LAD detects on its own generator, v4 V9).

(rows.matrices; as step 11's lap_top: isolated vertices add zeros only; zeros fill when the active part has fewer
eigenvalues): dense eigvalsh if the active part has < 2,000 vertices, else eigsh(which="LA", k=499, tol 1e-6).

Check M0: the top 5 of the new eigenvalues against LAD's stored input (max abs and relative difference), and the
EP of LAD recomputed with k = 5 from them against the Table 2 LAD row (pooled; per dataset).
Reported (step 23 part LAD): EP on all ten and >= 10 events (equal weight; two-stage CI; event-weighted), counts -
row and row - lad (two-stage CI, p), per-dataset EP; and the Holm F2 p-values with lad_k499 (resp. lad_k6) in place
of lad (one extra pre-specified computation, the reference row stays out of the main family).
Reading: if counts - lad_k499 (or lad_k6) has a two-stage interval below 0 the paper says that LAD with more
eigenvalues beats the counts; if lad_k499 - lad has a two-stage interval above 0, the paper says LAD's benchmark
EP is depressed by the 5-eigenvalue setting; otherwise, that the setting does not change LAD's standing.
